Delete your account

Last updated: October 11, 2026

You can permanently delete your GameFold account at any time. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — all of your personal data and user-generated content will be removed from our systems.

What gets deleted Your sign-in account (email address and, if you used Google, the name and profile picture link Google shared), your profile (username, bio, avatar choice), date of birth, settings and consent choices, push notification token, game library and ratings, reviews, lists, follows, likes, blocks, reports you filed, and the notifications you received. Deleting your sign-in account deletes everything linked to it in our database.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open GameFold and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (profile tab → gear icon).
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm with Delete Permanently.

Your data is removed immediately and you're signed out. You can create a new account later with the same email if you change your mind.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email (for users without the app installed)

If you can't or don't want to install the app, send a deletion request from the email address associated with your account:

  1. Email privacy@gamefold.app from your account's email address.
  2. Subject line: Account deletion request.
  3. Include your GameFold username (optional but speeds things up).
  4. We'll verify ownership of the email and delete your account within 30 days, as required by GDPR Art. 17. In practice most requests are processed within 72 hours.

Delete part of your data (and keep your account)

You don't have to delete your account to remove some of your data. In the app you can:

  • Reviews: open one of your reviews and delete it.
  • Lists: delete a whole list, or edit it to remove games from it.
  • Follows and likes: unfollow people or remove your like at any time.
  • Bio and avatar: edit or clear your bio, or switch your avatar, from your profile.
  • Usage analytics and crash reports: turn them off in Settings > Privacy & Safety. Nothing new is sent after that; what was already sent expires as described in our Privacy Policy.

To delete anything else (for example, specific games in your library) without deleting your account, email privacy@gamefold.app from your account's email address and tell us what to remove. We'll do it within 30 days.

Retention after deletion

  • Personal data in our active databases: removed immediately on deletion.
  • Encrypted backups: purged within 30 days of deletion during regular backup rotation.
  • Usage analytics and crash reports (PostHog, Sentry): they are linked only to a random ID, never to your email or username. They are not removed when you delete your account; they expire under the retention periods in our Privacy Policy.
  • Legal obligations: if we are required by Spanish or EU law to retain specific data (e.g., abuse-related reports for 12 months), we will notify you and retain only the minimum legally required.

What is NOT deleted

Notifications your activity created in other people's inboxes (for example, "… started following you") stay there, but without your name: they show "Someone" instead. Your username is released and may be registered by another user in the future.

Questions

For anything related to your data rights under GDPR/LOPDGDD, including access, rectification, portability, or objections, email privacy@gamefold.app. You may also file a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).
