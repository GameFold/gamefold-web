Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Summary We collect the personal data needed to run GameFold — your email, username, date of birth (to check you are at least 13), and anything you create in the app. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and a link to your Google profile picture. Crash reports are on by default, never include your email or username, and you can turn them off; usage analytics are off unless you opt in. You can delete your account and all your data at any time.

1. Data Controller

GameFold ("we", "us", "our") is responsible for the processing of your personal data.

Contact: privacy@gamefold.app

For any privacy-related inquiries, data access requests, or complaints, you may contact us at the email above.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Account data (provided by you)

  • Email address — for authentication and account recovery
  • Username — public identifier within the app
  • Date of birth — to check that you are at least 13 (COPPA)
  • Profile bio and avatar — optional. Your avatar is a generated image or an emoji you pick; the app has no photo upload.

From Google (only if you sign in with Google)

  • Your name and a link to your Google profile picture — Google shares them when you sign in, and they are stored with your account by our authentication provider (Supabase)

User-generated content

  • Game reviews, ratings, and lists you create
  • Your game library (the games you add, their status and your rating). It is private unless you make it public: the app asks after you add your first game, and you can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Safety. Even when it is private, other users can see how many games you are playing and have finished, but not which ones.
  • Follow relationships and social interactions

Automatically collected data

  • Crash and error reports (Sentry) — on by default, you can turn them off in Settings. A report contains the error and its stack trace, device model, operating system, app version, the recent in-app steps that led to the error, and a random identifier. It never contains your email, username, IP address, screenshots, or recordings of your screen.
  • Usage analytics (PostHog) — only if you opt in. Events such as viewing a game, adding a game to your library, or creating a review or list, linked to your random account ID and the date your account was created, plus device model, operating system, and app version. PostHog may use the IP address of the request to estimate your approximate location (such as your country or city). We never send your email to PostHog.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or files from your device, or financial information. The only location data is the approximate location PostHog may estimate from your IP address, and only if you opt in to usage analytics.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the GDPR and the Spanish LOPDGDD, we process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance (Art. 6.1.b GDPR): Account data and user-generated content are necessary to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR): Usage analytics (PostHog) are only active if you opt in. You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings without affecting the core service.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR): Crash and error reports (Sentry) without personal identifiers, to keep the app stable; basic security measures and abuse prevention. You can turn crash reports off at any time in Settings.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and maintain the GameFold service (account, reviews, lists, social features)
  • Authenticate you and protect your account
  • Enforce the minimum age of 13
  • Fix crashes and errors (unless you turn crash reports off)
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the experience (only with your consent)
  • Send push notifications (only if you grant permission)
  • Detect and prevent abuse or misuse of the service

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase (database & authentication) — your account data and content are stored on Supabase infrastructure. Servers located in the EU/US depending on project configuration.
  • PostHog (product analytics) — receives usage events linked to a random account ID, never your email. Only active if you opt in. Privacy: posthog.com/privacy
  • Sentry (error tracking) — receives crash and error reports without personal identifiers. On by default; you can turn it off in Settings. Privacy: sentry.io/privacy
  • RAWG API (game data) — we query game information on your behalf; no personal data is shared with RAWG.
  • Google Sign-In (optional authentication) — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google's privacy policy applies to the authentication flow.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers (Supabase, PostHog, Sentry) may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). When this occurs, we ensure adequate safeguards are in place, such as:

  • EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • The service provider's adherence to recognized data protection frameworks

By using GameFold, you acknowledge that some data may be processed outside the EEA under these safeguards.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained as long as your account is active. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • User-generated content (reviews, lists): Retained as long as your account is active. Permanently deleted upon account deletion via cascade.
  • Analytics data: Linked only to a random account ID (pseudonymous). Individual event data is not retained beyond 90 days.
  • Error logs: Retained for up to 30 days in Sentry, then automatically purged.
  • Push notification tokens: Cleared upon sign-out or account deletion.

When you delete your account, all associated personal data is permanently and irreversibly removed from our systems. See our account deletion page for details.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and Spanish LOPDGDD, you have the following rights:

  • Access — View your data directly in the app (profile, reviews, lists, settings).
  • Rectification — Edit your profile, reviews, and lists at any time.
  • Erasure ("Right to be forgotten") — Delete your account permanently from Settings. All data is cascade-deleted.
  • Data portability — Request an export of your data by contacting us.
  • Withdraw consent / opt out — Turn off usage analytics or crash reports at any time in Settings > Privacy & Safety.
  • Restriction of processing — Contact us to request restriction.
  • Object — Turn off crash reports in Settings, or contact us to object to any other processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Lodge a complaint — You may file a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD) at www.aepd.es or with your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app features or contact us at privacy@gamefold.app. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

GameFold is not intended for children under 13. We enforce a minimum age of 13 during registration (COPPA compliance).

GameFold does not show sexually explicit games to anyone.

If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete that data. If you believe a child under 13 is using GameFold, please contact us.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) on all database tables
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth
  • Session token management with automatic refresh

No system is 100% secure. If you discover a security vulnerability, please contact us responsibly at privacy@gamefold.app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top
  • Notify you in the app (via the legal acceptance modal if major changes require re-consent)

Continued use of GameFold after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

For any questions, data requests, or concerns about your privacy:

Email: privacy@gamefold.app

You may also exercise your rights by using the account management features in Settings.
